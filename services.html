COMPLETE SERVICE DIRECTORY

Every Scan & Procedure, Under One Roof.

All antenatal ultrasound scans and invasive fetal procedures available at our Punjabi Bagh centre, performed by Dr. Rachna Gupta.

9 SPECIALIZED SCANS

Antenatal Ultrasound

  1. 01
    Viability Scan

    Early pregnancy confirmation, fetal heartbeat and accurate dating.

  2. 02
    First Trimester Screening

    NT scan and biochemistry for aneuploidies, anomalies and pregnancy complications.

  3. 03
    Early Anomaly / Risk Reassessment Scan

    Focused early anatomy review with a revised risk assessment.

  4. 04
    Anomaly Scan (Level II / TIFFA)

    Detailed structural evaluation of the baby at 18–22 weeks.

  5. 05
    Dedicated Fetal Neurosonography

    High-resolution imaging of the fetal brain and spine.

  6. 06
    Fetal Echocardiography

    Detailed screening of the fetal heart for congenital defects.

  7. 07
    Transvaginal Cervical & Pre-eclampsia Screening

    Cervical length measurement with maternal risk assessment.

  8. 08
    Fetal Well-being Scan

    Growth, Doppler, AFI and biophysical profile for at-risk pregnancies.

  9. 09
    Multiple Pregnancy Scan

    Specialized monitoring for twins and triplets.

7 PROCEDURES

Invasive Tests

  1. 01
    Amniocentesis

    Amniotic fluid sampling for chromosomal and genetic testing.

  2. 02
    Amniodrainage

    Controlled drainage of excess amniotic fluid (polyhydramnios).

  3. 03
    Chorionic Villous Sampling (CVS)

    Early placental sampling for genetic diagnosis.

  4. 04
    Cordocentesis

    Fetal blood sampling for diagnosis and intrauterine treatment.

  5. 05
    Fetal Reduction

    Selective reduction in higher-order multiple pregnancies.

  6. 06
    Intrauterine Transfusion

    Treatment of severe fetal anaemia, including Rh incompatibility.

  7. 07
    Shunt Insertion (Bladder & Thorax)

    Shunt placement for bladder or pleural fluid collections.

Performed by Dr. Rachna Gupta All invasive procedures are carried out under continuous ultrasound guidance with complete fetal medicine backup.
